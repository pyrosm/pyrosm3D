# Changelog

## Unreleased

- NEW: The first version of pyrosm3d draws the OpenStreetMap elements that pyrosm reads with their levels in 3D, with plotly. `read_indoor(osm)` reads the indoor elements of the area of a `pyrosm.OSM` object into GeoDataFrames in metres: the indoor areas, the platform areas and the other areas that lines of the walking network cross, such as squares and station halls, with a kind (room, corridor, shaft, toilets, shop, platform, walking area, level outline, wall) and their levels, the walls, the doors, the shops and amenities that have a level, the building outlines and the walking network. `plot_indoor(indoor, level=None)` draws them as a figure: floors 4 m apart, open where stairs, an escalator or a moving walkway rises through them; walls with a gap at each door, cut away at 1.6 m; outer walls along level or building outlines; stairwells and lift shafts as columns; the walking network; and doors and shops as markers. `to_html(indoor, path)` writes the figure as a web page with a button for each level and a panel for the OpenStreetMap tags of a clicked element. `plot_network(edges, nodes)` draws a walking network alone, each edge at its levels; it is pyrosm's unreleased `plot_3d`, moved here, and draws moving walkways in a colour of their own. pyrosm3d needs a pyrosm that reads indoor elements (`get_network(..., indoor_elements=True)`), which no pyrosm release has yet ([#1](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/1), [#2](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/2), [#3](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/3), [#4](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/4), [#5](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/5), [#6](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/6), [#7](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/7), [#8](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/8), [#9](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/9), [#10](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/10), [#11](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/11), [#14](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/14), [#16](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/16))
