# Changelog

## Unreleased

- NEW: The first version of pyrosm3d draws the OpenStreetMap elements that pyrosm reads with their levels in 3D, with plotly. It gives pyrosm's `OSM` object the accessor `in3d`, whose views read what they draw with pyrosm: the indoor elements with the walking areas (`get_indoor_elements(walking_areas=True)`), the walking network with `indoor_elements=True` and the buildings. `osm.in3d.plot_indoor(level=None)` draws the indoor elements as a figure: floors 4 m apart, open where stairs, an escalator or a moving walkway rises through them; walls with a gap at each door, cut away at 1.6 m; the outlines of levels, of buildings that hold indoor areas and of station buildings; outer walls along level or building outlines; stairwells and lift shafts as columns; the walking network; and doors and shops as markers. The floors are the indoor areas, the platform areas, the walking areas, such as squares and station halls, and the other areas that lines of the walking network cross, each with a kind (room, corridor, shaft, toilets, shop, platform, walking area, level outline, wall) and its levels. `osm.in3d.to_html(path)` writes the figure as a web page with a button for each level, which names the number of areas on it, and a panel that says what a clicked element is and lists its OpenStreetMap tags. `osm.in3d.plot_network()` draws the walking network alone, each edge at its levels, also a graph of `get_graph(indoor_elements=True)` with a route; it is pyrosm's unreleased `plot_3d`, moved here, and draws moving walkways in a colour of their own. The functions `plot_indoor(elements, edges, nodes, buildings, level=None)`, `to_html(elements, path, edges, nodes, buildings)` and `plot_network(edges, nodes)` draw frames that were read with pyrosm, also after they were changed: the indoor elements (walls, doors, and the shops and amenities that have a level among them), the walking network and the building outlines. pyrosm3d needs a pyrosm that has `OSM.in3d` and `get_indoor_elements`, which no pyrosm release has yet ([#1](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/1), [#2](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/2), [#3](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/3), [#4](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/4), [#5](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/5), [#6](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/6), [#7](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/7), [#8](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/8), [#9](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/9), [#10](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/10), [#11](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/11), [#14](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/14), [#16](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/16), [#18](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/18), [#20](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/20), [#22](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/22), [#24](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/24), [#26](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/26), [#28](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/28), [#29](https://github.com/pyrosm/pyrosm3D/pull/29))
