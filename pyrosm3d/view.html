What this is and how to move
  • @@ABOUT@@
  • One finger moves the view along the ground. A pinch zooms, and twisting the two fingers turns it. Three fingers moved up or down tilt it, and so does the slider.
  • The view tilts all the way: from straight above, past the side, to below the ground. Seen from the side, one finger moves the view up and down as well.
  • With a mouse: drag to move and scroll to zoom. To turn and tilt, drag with Shift held or with the right button, or use the Turn and Tilt sliders.
  • The views: "Buildings" is the solid model, where the ground and buildings hide what is inside and below them. "See through" turns the model into a map, where the walkways inside buildings and below ground show; those below ground are paler.
  • A view with indoor elements also has "Indoor": the indoor mapping alone, without the ground. The floors are plates, one above the other. "Level" shows one level alone. Lifts and moving walkways are dashed lines. Layers that are off at the start are in "Layers".
  • "True heights": on, heights are drawn to scale; off, they are drawn taller, so that what lies above and below each other stands apart. "Buildings" and "See through" start with it on, "Indoor" with it off; each remembers its own choice.
  • The Tilt and Turn sliders tilt and turn the view; the buttons below them look from above, from the side, with north up, or go back to the start.
  • Tap a line or a floor to read what it is.
